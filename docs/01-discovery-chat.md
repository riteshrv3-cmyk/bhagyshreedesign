# Bhagyshree Design: Discovery Chat

Date: 2026-10-02

## Goal
Set up a digital presence for a fashion design business (Bhagyshree Design, run by the owner's mother): Instagram account, website, Google Business Profile, plus all content.

## Questions and answers

### Round 1: Business basics
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What does she make or sell? | Custom stitching, ethnic and bridal, ready-made designs, alterations and boutique |
| Where do customers come from today? | Local area only |
| Where does she work from? | Home studio |
| Main goal of the digital presence? | Get more enquiries |

### Round 2: Brand and look
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Brand feel | Rich and festive (deep reds, golds, bold Indian colours) |
| Logo, colours, name style | Nothing yet, needs everything from scratch |
| Photos of her work | Some (10 to 30, mixed quality) |
| Language | Marathi and English |

### Round 3: Running it
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How customers contact her | WhatsApp, phone call, Instagram DM |
| Who posts on Instagram | Scheduled in advance for her |
| Budget | Free only |
| Where to start | Recommend an order |

## Recommended plan
1. **Brand basics (day 1):** name style, colours, fonts, simple logo idea.
2. **Google Business Profile (days 1 to 3):** home address can be hidden and a service area shown instead. Verification can take a few days, so start first.
3. **Website (days 2 to 5):** one fast page with her work, services, story, and big WhatsApp and Call buttons. Free hosting, free address for now, Marathi and English.
4. **Instagram (days 3 to 7):** bio, highlights, first 12 posts, 30-day calendar with captions in both languages.

## Who does what
- Claude: all text, website build, post and caption plan, poster and post designs.
- Owner or mother: create the accounts and complete Google verification (passwords and codes are entered by them only).

## Still needed from the owner
1. Exact spelling of the name: "Bhagyshree Design" or "Bhagyshree Designs"
2. Area and city, phone number, WhatsApp number
3. Prices or price range for main items, working hours
4. Her story: years of experience, specialities, customer feedback
5. Photos, placed in the `photos` folder of this repo
